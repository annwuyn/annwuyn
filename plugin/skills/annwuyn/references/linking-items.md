# Conjunctive linking items — master table

219 entries, transcribed from the session-S18 handout *A Summary of Conjunctive Linking Items*.
Look an item up here **before** using it. Most Vietnamese-English linking errors are
role errors (using an adverb where a conjunction is needed) or relation errors
(using a CONTRAST item where EXCLUSION is meant).

**Role** — `conj/co` coordinating conjunction · `conj/sub` subordinating conjunction ·
`conj inf` conjunction + infinitive · `correlative conj` correlative pair · `adv` adverb ·
`prep` preposition.
Grammatical weight: `conj/sub` and `prep` **subordinate**; `conj/co` and `adv` **coordinate**.

**Reg.** (register/tone) — `N` normal · `F` formal · `A` academic · `L` literary ·
`I` informal · `S` slang/spoken · `O` old/dated · `LW` legal writing ·
`ON` old-fashioned nuance · `BR`/`AM` British/American.

Same item, different role → often a different relation and a different dependency
structure. `BUT` as conj/co = CONTRAST; `BUT` as prep = EXCLUSION.
`BESIDES` as amplification ≠ `BESIDES` as exclusion. Always check the row you need.
