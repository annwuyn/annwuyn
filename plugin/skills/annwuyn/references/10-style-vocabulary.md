# 10 — Academic vocabulary, style and political correctness

Source: session S31.

## Near-synonym pairs to get right

The course's homework list — the second member is the academic choice in most contexts:

| Everyday | Academic |
|---|---|
| about, around | approximately |
| almost | virtually |
| basically | essentially |
| exact(ly) | precise(ly), accurate(ly) |
| finally | eventually, ultimately |
| importance | significance |
| important | significant, critical |
| in more detail | in greater detail |
| understanding | insight |
| just | merely |
| last | final(ly) |
| mainly | primarily |
| more than | in excess of, exceed |
| obtain | acquire |
| often | frequently |
| only | sole(ly) |
| purpose | end |
| (to) record | (to) document |
| result | outcome |
| size | magnitude |
| try | attempt |

Also: *first, second, last* vs *firstly, secondly, lastly* (and the *last but not least*
cliché); *deadly / lethal / fatal*; *aforementioned* [F] vs *aforesaid* [N→F];
*apparently* vs *clearly*; *assumption* [N] vs *supposition* [F].

## Avoid overusing be / have / get / do / thing

| Weak | Stronger |
|---|---|
| This **is** still a controversial issue | This issue **remains** controversial |
| could **be a problem** of hygiene | could **pose** some hygiene problems (also *pose a challenge \| threat \| risk \| danger \| obstacle*) |
| can **be a good source of making** communication | would **constitute** a good source for **stimulating** communication |
| people cannot **get good food** easily | people would not **have ready access to quality food** |
| **gives** readers the various theories | **informs** readers of the various theories |
| before coming to one conclusion | in order to **form an informed opinion** |
| This **thing** may put a child in a difficult situation | This **delusion \| misconception** may… |
| the same **stuff** | the same **merchandise or service** |
| a tough **situation** for the mayor | a tough **proposition** for the mayor |
| contribute **much more** than artists | contribute **substantially more** than artists |
| Children here | **Local** children |
| **is due to** three main factors | **is ascribed to \| is attributable to** three main factors |
| an issue that we can see every day | we can observe … **on a daily basis** |
| stories … he did not know whether it was exact | stories **whose accuracy could not be verified** |
| a product's **gorgeous appearance** | a product's **aesthetic appeal** |
| the evidence is not good enough to protect your claim | the evidence is not **sufficiently legitimate to justify** your claim |
| helped me get over the **melancholy** | helped me get over **this negative feeling** |
| **Noticeably**, the new method is **firstly welcomed** by the young who can help the older **approach** this | **It is noteworthy that** the new method is **first embraced** by the younger generation, who **subsequently help the older acquaint themselves with it** |
| The point is **totally disagreeable** | The point is **debatable \| disputable at best** |
| seems his **pure imagination**. **As an example**… | seems **purely speculative**. **As a counterexample**… |

Watch for over-reach in the other direction: *Setting your goals low **ameliorates** your
problem-solving skills* → ***compromises***. Big words help, but only the right ones.

## Political correctness

> *Did you just call Dad stupid without calling him stupid?* — the summary of PC in academia.

| Blunt | PC |
|---|---|
| a boring teacher | an **uninspiring** teacher |
| poor countries like Vietnam | **lower-middle-income** countries such as Vietnam (the World Bank group; *developing* is the course’s 2010s choice, now judged inaccurate, see `13` §10) |
| poor and dirty people | **underprivileged \| disadvantaged** people |
| a country well-known for its **repulsive** cuisine | …for its **unique** cuisine |
| An example of **disgusting** dishes | An example of dishes **not to everyone's liking** |

**Negation and indirectness help:**
- *I had to go to a bad school* → *I **did not have the opportunity to attend a better** school*
- *These apps are not good enough* → *These apps are **mediocre at best***
- *How good is your English? Not so good* → ***I'm working on it.*** (interview tip)
- *He hesitates to talk to ugly women* → *He is **reluctant** to talk to **less attractive**
  women*

## Style traps

- **Don't name-drop.** Listing famous names you merely attended a lecture by adds nothing and
  reads as padding.
- **Don't rely on templates.** *Many studies demonstrate | prove | report | point out | suggest |
  claim | conclude that…* — each of these is a different Engagement move (see
  `06-engagement.md`); template overreliance flattens them into noise. The same holds for
  phrasebank frames: `12-phrasebank.md` → *Choosing between candidate frames* is the antidote
  (Theme fit, Engagement fit, force fit, economy, variety, genre).
- **Check factual labels.** *Vietnamese Animal Association* → *Animal **Protection**
  Association*; *sign language* → *body language*; *marketing **tragedies*** → *strategies*.
- **Check the term against the culture you invoke.** *While Americans eat a lot of beef, Indian
  Hindus **worship beef*** — worship the cow, not the beef.
