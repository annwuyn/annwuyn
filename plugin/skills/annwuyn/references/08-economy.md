# 8 — Economy of language

Source: sessions S25, S29. The compression toolkit. Applied in order, a 25–50 % cut is normal
without losing content — the course routinely demonstrates 667→500 and 70→35 words.

## The nine principles

1. Linearity, scope & distance ← already covered; fixing these alone removes most bloat
2. Redundancy-free
3. Default value
4. Noun Phrase (NP)
5. Affix
6. Concise term
7. Formal lexis
8. Idiom
9. Sentence complexity

---

### 2. Redundancy-free

- *people **seem to** be so busy* · *In my opinion, I agree with…* · *From my perspective, I
  strongly support…* · *I individually assume that…*
- redundancy in speaking ≠ redundancy in writing
- *in order to have well-paid jobs **and good salary*** (redundancy vs linearity)
- *I suggested that she **can** choose another **easier** subject*
- *more **additional** evidence* · *collaborate … **together*** · *most **favorite***
- ***Thanks to the aid of** modern technologies* → *Thanks to modern technologies*
- **word-level linearity**: *They consider dogs **as** their most favorite pets.*

Vietnamese-specific reading: *Tiếng Việt đang dài ra* (Nguyễn Đức Dân) — Sino-Vietnamese +
native doublets (*tái xuất hiện trở lại*), self-evident elements, and chained synonyms
(*chưa từng xảy ra bao giờ từ trước đến nay*) transfer straight into English drafts.

### 3. Default value

Delete what the word already entails.
- *A **low-income** person could eventually afford a big house simply by saving **his** money.*
- *He has no evidence to prove what he said **was true**.*
- *a dramatic increase … in recent years **compared to the past***
- *He claims that Vietnamese eat **meat from** birds and dogs*
- *Scandals have become a **controversial** topic*
- *Matt's argument … is **arguable** to me*
- *a good **advice*** · *there are many kids who don't have a chance … **because of many
  reasons*** → *many kids in Vietnam don't have a chance…*

### 4. Noun Phrase

Identify the head noun and **condense information into NPs** rather than spreading it over a
whole sentence. Brevity vs linking distance — the NP wins.

| Long | NP |
|---|---|
| the relationship among members in families | among **family members** |
| more than people in other countries | than **other nationalities** |
| graded in the evaluation at the end of the year | in the **end-of-year \| year-end** evaluation |
| children between the ages of 6 and 18 … something not suitable for their ages | **school-age children** … **age-inappropriate activities** |
| diet that contains a lot of protein taken from meat | **protein-rich \| high-protein**, **meat-based \| meat-dominant** diet |
| The pool of high quality human resources … is limited and extends very slowly | The **low-growth pool** … is limited |
| Việc đầu tư … được thực hiện một cách bài bản và lâu dài | Our **long-term investment** will be **systematically** delivered |

Vietnamese uses PPs to modify nouns; English prefers pre-modification.

### 5. Affix

| Long | Affixed |
|---|---|
| his trip of 10 days throughout Vietnam | his **10-day trans-Vietnam** trip |
| information sharing within / between the sectors | **intra-sector \| inter-sector** information sharing |
| occurred before war / after graduation | **pre-war \| post-graduation** event |
| group D consisting of three subjects | **tri-subject** group D |
| the cities that are easy to walk around | **walking-friendly** cities |
| the region where most residents are farmers | the **agro-centric** region |
| We both signed the document together | **co-sign** |
| gave him wrong advice | **misadvise** |

Families to exploit: `pro-/anti-` · `pseudo-` [negative] vs `quasi-` [neutral] · `semi-` ·
`non-` · `co-` · `mis-` · `-free` (pollution-free, ad-free, Covid-free) · `-friendly` ·
`-based` · `-less` · `-phobe/-phile/-maniac` (*Sinophobe*, *Koreaphile*, *IELTS-mania*).

Affixes help with NP formation. Root reference: `learnthat.org/pages/view/roots.html`.

**Notable exception** — sometimes V + NP is the *more* academic choice, not V + Adv:
*describe* → *provide a detailed description of* · *explain* → *offer a well thought-out
explanation for* · *explore* → *carry out a long-term exploration of*.

### 6. Concise term

Re-define with the existing term instead of paraphrasing around it.

| Long | Term |
|---|---|
| inevitable to when anyone travels to anywhere | inevitable **to travelers** |
| clubs of those sharing the same interest | **hobby clubs** |
| misunderstanding between international colleagues because of cultural difference | **cross-cultural miscommunication** |
| fights against opponents who wanted to conquer our country | **self-defense** |
| bridge between enterprises with recruitment demands and students … with little experience | bridge between **recruiting businesses** and the **entry-level workforce** |
| based on personal observations, unofficial interviews, stories from others and Internet hearsay | based on **anecdotal sources** |

Know the trio: **research-based** vs **anecdotal** vs **empirical** evidence.

### 7. Formal lexis

| Long | Formal |
|---|---|
| have chances to live with and contact with foreigners | **first-hand exposure to** foreigners |
| grave mistakes that we can't or hardly repair | **irreversible** mistakes |
| the way you call yourself | **self-proclaimed \| self-professed** |
| ingrained with inhumanity and savage persecution the same as the Nazi | as **genocidal** as the Nazi |
| could be futile, or even shorten our life instead of lengthening it | or even **counter-productive** |

### 8. Idiom

| Long | Idiom |
|---|---|
| see everything at different angles to give a right evaluation | **put everything in perspective** |
| could get either a bad or good influence from this early time | **for better or for worse** |
| as live or dead matter | **as a matter of life and death** |

### 9. Sentence complexity — the complex sentence principle

Tools: modifiers (NP, PP, AdvP) and clauses — which also improve TP and dependency.

- *Some people agree with this opinion **because they argue that**…* [over-linking]
  → *Some people **advocate** this view, **arguing that**…* [non-restrictive, reduced]
- *required further investigations on this issue. **Investigations** subsequently revealed
  that…* [R-T pattern] → *further investigations on this issue, **which revealed that**…*
- *However, she always thinks positive. She learnt how to write by using her mouth…* [T-T,
  dependency issue] → *However, she, **always someone with positive thinking** [NP modifier, TP
  downgrading], learned how to write using her mouth and a special pen.*

## Worked example

> *Some people may disagree with the statement [S1]. **Specifically**, they claim that
> [over-linking] we should stick to **possible goals within our reach** [word-level linearity]
> [S2]. **As they often believe** [redundancy], the argument for this claim is that [complex
> sentence] people may waste a great deal of time for **pursuing something they hope to
> achieve** [concise term], **which appears to be impossible** [NP] [S3]. Especially, they, in
> some cases, may spend their whole life for such a goal [lexical Rheme-Rheme, downgrading],
> **which is even more unfortunate** [NP] [S4].* — 70 words

> **Some people may disagree with the statement, claiming that we should stick to realistic
> goals, lest we waste a great deal of time, or worse in some cases, our whole life, on pursuing
> out-of-reach targets.** — 35 words, 50 % cut

## In scholarship essays

Being personal ≠ being literary. Cut the purple:
*Go face the force of the wind, and fight the slash of the rain. The palm of your hands will
thicken… This is my motto and I try to live by it each day.*

A 98-word paragraph compressed to 59 (40 % cut) by NP-ing the topic, reducing a clause, and
merging two sentences. Extreme compressions are possible where the content is strong:

> *Strong backgrounds in Math and Statistics. Valedictorian from a university first-ranked in
> Finance. CFA-II certified. One year working as ABC's consultant. I'm ready for my next
> prerequisite step toward my career goals — a Master's degree in XYZ Science, not yet available
> in Vietnam.* — 44 words, a 70 % cut

> *As the adopted daughter in a family of a HUMBLE social background, it was much harder for me
> to make it in the dominantly PATRIARCHAL and ELITIST society of Vietnam. But I did…*
> — 33 words replacing 168
